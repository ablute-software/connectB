Questions

Frequently asked

Is my deal flow private?

Yes. Your mandate and the startups matched to it are private to your organisation by default. It is never used to grow the marketplace or train shared models without your explicit consent.

Who can see startup data?

Only verified investor organisations with a complete profile and an active mandate. Browsing your matches is private — founders aren't notified while you're exploring. When you want to go further, introductions happen on MatchDeal, our companion discovery app: the startup decides, and only a mutual opt-in opens identity or a data room.

How do you verify investors?

Work email and domain for collaboration; stronger proof — including official registry checks — for organisation control and sensitive actions.

What does "qualified opportunity" mean?

A company that passed your mandate's hard criteria and received a full analysis: fit, evidence, risks and a recommended action. Raw names on a list do not count against your capacity.
