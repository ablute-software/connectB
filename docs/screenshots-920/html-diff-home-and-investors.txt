> <details class="landing_faqItem__19L1H">
> <summary>Can I compare startups side by side?</summary>
> <p>Yes. The comparison view puts startups next to each other on match score and the criteria behind it, round size and stage, together with your own scorecard and valuation estimate if you use them. Your scores and notes are private to your organisation; the founder never sees them.</p>
> </details>
> <details class="landing_faqItem__19L1H">
> <summary>Can I keep following startups I did not invest in, and re-evaluate them later?</summary>
> <p>Yes. Add a startup to your Watching list and choose what should bring it back to your attention: new evidence of traction, a new round or a changed target, a roadmap milestone, or a match score that rises above a level you set. The list, its order and the thresholds are private to you.</p>
