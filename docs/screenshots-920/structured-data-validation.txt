  /faq            2            Breadcrumbs, Organization                          Organization: streetAddress, postalCode
  /investors      3            Article, Breadcrumbs, Organization                 Article: image · Organization: streetAddress, postalCode

  Errors: none on any page. "Critical" problems: none.
  The optional fields are deliberately absent: the Organization carries the town only (Viana do Castelo) — the registered office, with street and postal code, is
  already public in the Terms (src/lib/controller.ts CONTROLLER_ADDRESS), so adding streetAddress/postalCode would disclose nothing new, but it is Nuno's call, not
  mine (listed as an optional "sim" in the report); no review/rating data (aggregateRating would be an invented number); no article image (the Open Graph image is
  decision D10, deferred).

  Fixed during this validation: the Article's datePublished/dateModified were the bare date "2026-10-16", which the tool reads as an invalid date-time without a time
  zone (4 optional-field notes). They are now ISO date-times, "2026-10-16T12:00:00Z" (noon UTC: the reviewed DAY is the fact, the time is nominal and noon keeps
  the calendar day in every time zone from UTC-12 to UTC+11). The Article went from 5 notes to 1 (image). site-schema.test.ts pins it.

  What this tool does NOT list, and why: FAQPage, HowTo and AboutPage. Google stopped producing FAQ rich results for ordinary sites in 2023 (it keeps them for
  well-known government and health sites) and removed HowTo rich results the same year; AboutPage has never been a rich-result type. They are in the markup as
  machine-readable structure for answer engines and any consumer of schema.org, not as a promise of a Google rich result (docs/site-copy-920.md §0, D9).
  Their syntax is what the second tool checks.
